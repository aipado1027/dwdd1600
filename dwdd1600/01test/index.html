Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means being a good person online, just like being a good citizen in real life. \

What is digital law?

Digital law sets the boundaries for acceptable behavior online, just like traditional laws do in the physical world.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy. It is a set of rules and guidelines that explain how computers, networks, internet access, and digital systems may be used properly and responsibly within an organization such as a school, company, or institution.

What is Plagerism?

Plagiarism is the act of using someone else’s work, ideas, or words and presenting them as your own without giving proper credit.

What is Acedemic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is any form of cheating or unfair behavior in an educational setting that gives a student an unearned advantage.

What Are the Rules For AI Generated Content?

Au-Generated Content (text, Images, audio, video) May be used only when allowed and only in Responsible ways, AI is a tool to support learning-not a replacement for thinking or original work.
