Decloration of Independents

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence (1776) include

John Hancock
President of the Continental Congress; known for his large, bold signature.
Thomas Jefferson
Main author of the Declaration of Independence.
Benjamin Franklin
Scientist, inventor, diplomat, and influential Founding Father.
John Adams
Strong supporter of independence; later became the 2nd President of the United States.
Samuel Adams
Leader in organizing resistance against British rule.
Roger Sherman
Only person to sign the Declaration of Independence, Articles of Confederation, and the U.S. Constitution.
Robert Morris
Wealthy merchant who helped finance the American Revolutionary War.
Charles Carroll of Carrollton
The only Catholic signer and one of the wealthiest men in the colonies.
